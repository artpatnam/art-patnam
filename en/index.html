Frequently asked questions

How does art rental work?

You tell us about your space and we propose a tailored selection of artworks, with no obligation. If it suits you, we take care of the installation and you pay a monthly fee. The contract lasts 5 months and, at the end, you decide whether to keep the same artworks, swap them for others or cancel.

What does the monthly fee include?

Everything: personalised selection, decor advice, delivery, installation and removal of the artworks, support throughout the contract, collection renewal and the purchase option. There are no set-up costs or extra charges.

How long is the contract and what happens at the end?

The contract lasts 5 months. At the end you have three options: keep the same artworks, renew the collection with new ones or cancel your subscription. And if you fall in love with a piece, you can also buy it.

Can I cancel before the 5 months are up?

Yes, you can cancel whenever you need to. If you cancel mid-month, you only pay the proportional part of that month.

What happens if an artwork is damaged?

Every artwork is delivered in perfect condition and we help you choose a suitable spot for it. Even so, if an artwork is damaged during the rental, the client will be responsible for the cost of its repair or replacement, which will be assessed case by case depending on the piece and the extent of the damage.

Which areas do you cover?

Madrid and Valencia, where we handle delivery and installation of the artworks.
