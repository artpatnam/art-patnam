FAQ

Everything you need to know

Answers to the most common questions about renting art for your business.

The service

How does art rental work?

You tell us about your space and we propose a tailored selection of artworks, with no obligation. If it suits you, we take care of the installation and you pay a monthly fee. Each period lasts 5 months and, at the end, you decide whether to keep the same artworks, swap them for others or cancel.

What does the monthly fee include?

Everything: personalised selection, decor advice, delivery, installation and removal of the artworks, support throughout the whole period, collection renewal and the purchase option. There are no set-up costs or extra charges.

Which plan is right for me?

It depends on the size of your space and how many areas you want to dress. As a guide: Essential (3 artworks) for offices, cafés or hair salons; Professional (5 artworks) for clinics, restaurants and offices; Premium (7 artworks) for fine-dining restaurants and boutique hotels; and Impact (10 artworks) for hotels and large spaces. If you are unsure, we will recommend the best fit once we know your space.

Can I choose the artworks?

Yes. We propose a selection designed for your space and your brand, and fine-tune it with you until it fits. Besides the artworks on the website, more are available on request.

Are the artworks originals?

Yes, all of them. We work with original works by artists: oil paintings, mixed media and fine-art prints such as screen prints and lithographs. All of them come framed.

Do you work with freelancers or only with companies?

Both. We work with companies as well as freelancers and independent professionals: offices, clinics, studios, hair salons, shops…

Which areas do you cover?

Madrid and Valencia, where we handle delivery and installation of the artworks.

Periods and payments

How long is each period and what happens at the end?

Each period lasts 5 months, with no commitment. At the end you have three options: keep the same artworks, renew the collection with new ones or cancel your subscription. And if you fall in love with a piece, you can also buy it.

Can I cancel before the 5 months are up?

Yes. There’s no minimum commitment. If you decide to stop before the 5 months are up, you only pay for the time you’ve enjoyed the artworks, and we collect them at no extra cost.

How do I pay?

By direct debit. The payment is taken every month on the same day you signed the contract: for example, if you sign on the 10th, you are charged on the 10th of each month.

Can I buy an artwork? How much does it cost?

Yes, every artwork can be purchased. The price depends on each piece, so if one catches your eye, get in touch and we will send you its documentation and price.

Installation and care

What does installation involve? Do you drill holes?

Our team takes care of everything, so you don’t have to lift a finger. How long it takes depends on the number of artworks and, depending on the type of piece, it may or may not be necessary to drill into the wall.

Can I change the artworks during the period?

Yes, as long as the artworks you want are available. That way you can refresh your space without waiting for the period to end.

What happens if an artwork is damaged?

Every artwork is delivered in perfect condition and we help you choose a suitable spot for it. Even so, if an artwork is damaged during the rental, the client will be responsible for the cost of its repair or replacement, which will be assessed case by case depending on the piece and the extent of the damage.
